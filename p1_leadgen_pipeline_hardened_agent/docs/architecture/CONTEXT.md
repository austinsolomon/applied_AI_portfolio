# Architecture glossary

- **Plan:** Approved city and keyword cells, raw targets and budget.
- **Run:** One execution identity retained through checkpoint recovery.
- **Cell:** A city and keyword combination assigned to a market.
- **Reservation:** Durable held budget for a paid operation whose final outcome may be unknown.
- **Checkpoint:** A saved successful stage result that resume can skip.
- **Raw lead:** A sourced business retained before dedupe and qualification.
- **Qualified:** Evidence supports eligible services and affirmative market coverage.
- **Review:** Evidence remains insufficient for a confident eligibility decision.
- **CRM lead:** A qualified, nonduplicate business with a usable contact, saved transactionally.
- **Eval label:** A human assessment of a captured decision, with an optional note.
- **Perspective:** One focused diagram of the same application; components can be shared across views.
